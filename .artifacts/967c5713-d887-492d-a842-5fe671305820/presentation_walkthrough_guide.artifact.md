# Remindly: Presentation Walkthrough Script

Use this guide to demonstrate **Remindly** during your presentation. It follows a logical flow from first opening the app to using its most advanced "Study Mission" features.

---

## **1. Introduction & Branding**
*   **The Hook**: "Welcome to Remindly, your all-in-one student hub. As you can see, we’ve moved away from boring, corporate designs in favor of a **'So Matcha' Neo-brutalist aesthetic**—bold lines, high contrast, and a playful retro vibe that makes productivity feel less like a chore."
*   **Splash & Welcome**: Show the clean entry screens. "The Ivory 'Paper Style' background provides a calm starting point for a busy student's day."

## **2. The Dashboard (The "Student Brain")**
*   **XP & Rank**: Point out the level system. "To keep students motivated, we use a gamified **XP system**. Completing tasks earns you experience, helping you climb from a 'Novice Scholar' to a 'Grand Scholar'."
*   **Smart Hub**: Show 'Today's Mission' and 'Upcoming Reviews'. "The dashboard automatically pulls the most urgent class and study session, so the user knows exactly where they need to be right now."

## **3. Academic Organization (Subjects)**
*   **Subject List**: Navigate to the **Subjects** tab.
*   **Action**: Briefly open a subject. "Every subject is a folder for your academic life, storing its own teachers, rooms, and study materials."

## **4. The Full-Week Schedule (New Feature!)**
*   **Full Week**: Navigate to **Class Schedule**.
*   **Highlight**: "We support the full **7-day week** in a clean, scrollable selector. Whether you have Saturday labs or Sunday seminars, Remindly has you covered."
*   **Double Notifications**: Mention the alarms. "Unlike most apps, Remindly uses a **Double Notification system**. It alerts you 15 minutes before class *and* exactly when it starts, ensuring you're never late."

## **5. The Study Mission (Focus Mode)**
*   **Focus Tool**: Navigate to the **Study Mission** or App Blocking settings.
*   **The Demo**: "Students face constant distractions from social media. Our **App Locker** physically prevents you from opening apps like TikTok or Facebook during a study session."
*   **The Overlay**: "Try to open a blocked app, and our Neo-brutalist lock screen will appear, keeping you accountable until your 'Mission' is complete."

## **6. Advanced Review Sessions & Smart To-dos**
*   **Deep Dive**: Open a **Review Session** detail screen.
*   **Resources**: Show the Tabs (Notes, Files, Links). "You can attach lecture PDFs, save YouTube links, and write notes directly within a specific exam review session."
*   **Smart To-dos**: Create a "New To-do".
    *   "Our tasks are smarter than a simple checklist. You can add descriptions and sub-tasks."
    *   **Live Status**: Tap the badge to cycle through `Pending` -> `In Progress` -> `Completed`.
    *   **Automation**: "If a deadline passes, the app automatically marks the task as **'Missed'** in red, giving the user an honest look at their progress."

## **7. The Global Archive (Organization)**
*   **Clutter-Free**: Archive a finished subject.
*   **Recovery**: Navigate to **Settings > Archived Items**.
*   **Restore**: "We believe data integrity is key. You can hide old semesters to stay focused, but you can always **Restore** your notes and records from the archive whenever you need them."

---

## **Closing Statement**
"Remindly isn't just an app; it's a focus-engineered workspace designed for the modern student. It keeps your data organized, your distractions locked away, and your academic progress gamified. Thank you!"

---

> [!TIP]
> **Pro Presentation Tip**: Start a "Study Mission" with a 1-minute timer at the beginning of your demo. By the time you get to the end of your walkthrough, the "Mission Complete" notification will trigger naturally, providing a great "wow" moment for your audience!
