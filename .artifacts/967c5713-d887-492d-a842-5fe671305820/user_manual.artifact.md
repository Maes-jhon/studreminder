# Remindly: Professional User Manual (Activity 1-F)

Welcome to **Remindly**, your all-in-one academic hub. This manual will guide you through the features, navigation, and setup of the application to help you maximize your academic focus and organization.

---

## 1. Introduction & Purpose
**Remindly** is a focus-engineered student productivity tool designed with a unique **"So Matcha" Neo-brutalist** aesthetic. Unlike standard planners, Remindly combines academic organization (subjects, schedules, notes) with active distraction blocking (App Locker) and gamified motivation (XP/Rank system).

**Core Functions:**
*   **Centralize**: Keep all subjects, review sessions, and resources in one dashboard.
*   **Focus**: Physically block distracting apps during study "missions."
*   **Track**: Monitor task progress with smart status logic and automated "Missed" alerts.
*   **Archive**: Store old semester data safely without cluttering your workspace.

## 2. Getting Started

### **2.1 Registration & Login**
1.  Open the app to the **Welcome Screen**.
2.  Tap **Get Started** or **Register**.
3.  Enter your desired Username and Password.
4.  Once registered, log in to access your personal dashboard.

### **2.2 System Health & Permissions**
Remindly requires specific permissions to block apps and send reminders. If you see a **System Health Banner** on your dashboard, tap it to grant:
*   **Usage Access**: Allows the app to detect when a distracting app is opened.
*   **Overlay Permission**: Allows the "Study Mission" screen to appear on top of other apps.
*   **Notification/Alarm Permission**: Ensures you never miss a class.

## 4. Academic Organization

### **4.1 Managing Subjects**
1.  Navigate to the **Subjects** tab in the bottom bar.
2.  Tap the **(+) Add** button to create a new course.
3.  Enter the Subject Name, Teacher, and Room number.
4.  **Edit/Delete**: Long-press any subject card to update its details or remove it.

### **4.2 Class Schedule (The Full Week)**
1.  Navigate to the **Schedule** tab.
2.  Use the **Sun-Sat** selector at the top to view your agenda for any day of the week.
3.  Tap **Add Schedule** to link a class time to a subject.
4.  **Reminders**: Select a reminder offset (e.g., 15 mins before). Remindly uses a **Double Notification system**—it will alert you before class *and* exactly when it starts.

## 5. Review Sessions & Smart To-dos

### **5.1 Creating a Review Session**
1.  Open a **Subject** and tap **Add Review Session**.
2.  Set the topic, date, and time.
3.  The app will automatically schedule a notification to help you prepare.

### **5.2 Managing Resources (Tabs)**
Inside a Review Session, use the tabs to organize your materials:
*   **To-do**: Create smart tasks with descriptions and deadlines.
*   **Notes**: Write or import detailed study guides.
*   **Files**: Attach PDFs or lecture slides.
*   **Links**: Save YouTube tutorials or web resources.

### **5.3 Smart To-do Statuses**
Tap the **Status Badge** on any To-do to open the dropdown menu:
*   **Ongoing**: Task is currently being worked on (Blue icon).
*   **Completed**: Task is finished (Green icon).
*   **Missed**: Automatically triggered by the app if the deadline passes (Red icon).

## 6. The "Study Mission" (App Locker)
Block distractions and focus on your work.
1.  Go to **Settings > Blocked Apps**.
2.  Select the apps you want to restrict (e.g., TikTok, Facebook).
3.  Navigate to a **Review Session** and tap **Start Mission**.
4.  **The Lock**: If you attempt to open a blocked app, a Neo-brutalist overlay will block your screen. Enter your **Emergency PIN** (set in Settings) only if absolutely necessary.

---

## 7. The Global Archive
1.  Tap the **Archive (Box) icon** on any Subject or Review to hide it from your active list.
2.  To view or restore items, go to **Settings > Archived Items**.
3.  Tap the **Restore (Undo) icon** to move an item back to its original list.

---

## 8. Troubleshooting (Common Errors)

| Issue | Potential Cause | Solution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **App Locker not triggering** | Accessibility Service is OFF | Go to Settings > Accessibility and ensure "Remindly Focus Tracker" is **ON**. |
| **Notifications are late** | Battery Optimization | Tap the System Health banner and select "Ignore Battery Optimization." |
| **"Vanish" Confusion** | Item was Archived | Check **Settings > Archived Items** to restore your data. |
| **Honor/Huawei Issues** | App Launch Settings | Go to **Settings > App Launch** and set Remindly to "Manage Manually." |
